Audit my business funnel and suggest how to move people through it more effectively.

Context:
- use my current offer
- use my current funnel
- use my voice and positioning if you already know them
- if anything critical is missing, ask short follow-up questions first

Tasks:
1. Review the funnel from free value through paid offers.
2. Identify where people are likely dropping off.
3. Identify leaks in messaging, conversion, or follow-up.
4. Identify where the promise does not match the next step.
5. Suggest how to lead people more clearly from:
   - free offer to lead capture
   - lead capture to first sale
   - first sale to high-ticket offer
6. Suggest copy or messaging improvements where needed.
7. Suggest follow-up improvements where needed.
8. Rank the top fixes by likely revenue impact.

Output format:
- current funnel snapshot
- likely leaks
- likely reasons for the leaks
- recommended fixes
- quick wins this week
- bigger structural fixes later

Optimize for revenue and clarity, not complexity.
