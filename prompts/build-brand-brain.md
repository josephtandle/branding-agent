Use the Branding Agent to build a first-pass brand brain for this business.

Rules:
1. Ask me for my website if you do not already have it.
2. If I do not have a website, let me type or paste the offer instead.
3. Read the website or offer carefully before writing anything.
4. Use the frameworks in this repo when useful, especially:
   - awareness levels
   - competitive alternatives
   - mental vs visible market
   - value equation
   - jobs to be done
   - common enemy
5. Fill out a practical brand brain, not a fluffy one.
6. Keep your wording concrete enough that I could reuse it in copy, content, and sales.
7. Flag anything that is vague, generic, or unsupported.
8. End with:
   - the one-sentence positioning statement
   - the strongest promise
   - the likely audience awareness level
   - the biggest messaging gap
   - the 3 biggest open questions

If needed, use the template in `templates/brand-brain-template.md`.
