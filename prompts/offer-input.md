I want to clarify my offer before I improve my brand and funnel.

Do this in whichever way fits best:
- if I give you a webpage URL for my offer, study that page
- if I do not have a page, let me type or paste what my offer is

Then:
1. Summarize the offer in plain English.
2. Tell me who it is really for.
3. Tell me what result it promises.
4. Tell me what feels vague, generic, weak, or confusing.
5. Suggest a tighter version of:
   - the offer statement
   - the audience statement
   - the promise
   - the differentiator
6. Tell me what proof would make this easier to buy.
7. Give me 3 stronger positioning options, from safest to boldest.

Important:
- do not hallucinate services or outcomes
- if anything is unclear, ask a short follow-up question instead of guessing
- optimize for clarity and conversion, not cleverness
