Help me map my funnel clearly.

I want you to understand:
1. what I am giving away for free, if anything
2. what free offer or giveaway lets people experience me
3. what my first paid offer is
4. what my low-ticket offer is, if I have one
5. what my high-ticket offer is, if I have one
6. how I currently move people from one stage to the next

If you do not know my business yet, ask me for:
- my website or offer page
- a short description of what I sell
- my current funnel, even if it is messy

Then build a simple funnel map with these sections:
- traffic source
- first touch
- free value
- lead capture
- nurture step
- first sale
- ascension path
- high-ticket sale
- retention or repeat purchase

After mapping it:
1. tell me what is missing
2. tell me what is unclear
3. tell me where the funnel is too dependent on me manually
4. suggest the most important fixes in priority order

Keep the map practical. Use plain language.
