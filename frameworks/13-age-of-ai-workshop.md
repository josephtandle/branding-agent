# Framework: Age of AI Workshop — Part 1, 2, 3
> Source: Tiyana Ti, adapted for a workshop audience
> Live workshop page: https://ageofai.mastermindshq.business/
> Use for: running a full brand brain build from scratch for any project

---

## Series Overview

**Part 1 — Positioning:** Who you speak to and why they choose you
**Part 2 — Language:** How to say it so the right person feels it
**Part 3 — System:** Build the Brand Brain so AI works from your clarity, not the average

Each part builds on the previous. Part 1 must be solid before Part 2. Part 2 must be solid before Part 3.

---

## Part 1 Key Exercises (in order)

1. AI Description Test — paste current messaging, get a baseline before picture
2. Three Shifts and Two Markets — diagnose Visible vs Mental Market
3. The Full Through-Line — find the weakest link: Positioning / Language / Leads / System
4. Five Levels of Awareness — identify which level your audience is at
5. Client Behaviour Becomes Predictable — map what the ideal client does before finding you
6. The Client's Moment — describe your best-ever client specifically (not an imagined ideal)
7. Competitive Alternative Framework — what would they have done if they had never found you
8. Ferrari vs Toyota — are your sales conversations hard or easy?
9. The Common Enemy — what do you stand against?
10. The Positioning Statement — fill in the framework, run the specificity test
11. Anti-Positioning — who is this NOT for?
12. Positioning as Lead Generation System — connect positioning to content strategy

**Output:** Approved positioning statement that passes the specificity test.

## Part 2 Preview (Language)

Part 2 covers:
- Taking the positioning statement and turning it into outcome language
- The So What Chain in practice
- The Hormozi Value Equation applied to copy
- Visible Market vs Mental Market in actual sentences
- How to describe what you do so the right person immediately feels it

**Prerequisite:** Positioning statement from Part 1 must be approved.

## Part 3 Preview (System)

Part 3 covers:

- Training Claude on your positioning, voice, and ICP

**Prerequisite:** Language layer from Part 2 must be complete.
